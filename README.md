## About me

I build AI-enabled products and operations platforms with a focus on **LLM workflows, agentic systems, product strategy, automation, and measurable business outcomes**.

- **Founder — CareBow:** AI-powered healthcare coordination and symptom-assessment platform.
- **Builder — Mopshy AI:** AI automation, agent workflows, RAG systems, and growth infrastructure.
- **Product background:** discovery → PRD → roadmap → delivery → experimentation → iteration.
- **Core stack:** OpenAI, LangChain, Python, TypeScript, Next.js, Supabase, SQL, n8n, Make, APIs.
